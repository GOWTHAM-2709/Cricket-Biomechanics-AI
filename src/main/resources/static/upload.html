⚠️ Strict Recording Rules for Speed

Speed analysis uses physics displacement. Incorrect angles generate wildly wrong results.

  • Camera must be placed strictly side-on at a fixed distance.
  • Entire pitch distance must be clearly visible.
  • Use a fixed tripod. Zero camera movement.
  • High-frame-rate is strongly recommended.
